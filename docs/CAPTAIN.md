# Captain

Antonio Villalobos is the human captain and final authority for the trunk.

## Canon Authority

Changes to intent-tree, gardens, seeds, crew identity, and repository
graduation rules require captain approval before merge.

## Operating Principle

Agents may propose, scaffold, test, and repair. The captain decides when a
symbolic structure becomes canon and when a project graduates from nursery to
its own repository.

## Human Boundary

Clinical material, patient-related context, personal pillars, and private Drive
structure stay under captain authority and the relevant gatekeeper agents.
